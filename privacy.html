Sproutly

Your garden, your records.

Privacy notice · Updated November 3, 2026 · Pilot operated by Alambda Systems. Contact: jameshill@alambda.com.

Information we process

We store account name and email, protected password verification data, plants, seed projects, uploaded photos, journal entries, care conversations, growing plans, reminders and support notes to provide your account and plant-care features. Account records are separated by signed-in user. Session credentials are stored on your device; signing out removes the active local session.

AI and voice choices

When you consent to an AI feature, relevant photos, questions and growing history are sent to OpenAI. Recorded voice transcription sends audio to OpenAI; Sproutly does not retain that recording on its server. Live dictation uses your device or browser speech service, which may process audio online. Completed voice-call questions and plant context are sent to OpenAI. Text conversations and replies are saved to your account. Calls stop when you leave the screen or background the app. Provider handling and retention also follow the provider’s terms and your device settings.

Other services

Render hosts the pilot and persistent account database. Optional weather lookup shares approximate coordinates with Open-Meteo after consent. Botanical searches use GBIF; external merchant searches send search terms to the catalog service. Following a merchant link takes you to that merchant’s site and privacy practices. Marketplace demo orders are simulated.

Optional device notifications and camera, photo and microphone access are controlled through device permissions. Native digital subscriptions are not enabled for paid pilot access. If enabled in a future release, app-store and subscription-provider disclosures will be updated before launch.

Retention and deletion

Live account records remain until you delete the account. Deletion removes live account records, photos, conversations and sessions. Existing local backup snapshots expire through seven-snapshot rotation; deletion does not instantly erase those files. Verified recovery delivery and offsite backup are not yet enabled. Request timing logs contain request identifiers, method, status and latency, rather than question bodies, tokens, coordinates or photo content.

Voluntary beta feedback

If you volunteer or send feedback by email, we use your message to respond and review beta participation. If you choose to provide installation evidence, we may record a participant identifier, device platform, app version, acquisition channel, installation date and observed activation or return-use dates. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. Screenshots are optional and should hide personal details. Public launch reports use aggregate counts; downloads and website visits are not treated as verified installs. This page adds no advertising or automatic install-tracking SDK. You can request removal of your beta evidence by emailing the contact above.

Your choices

You can export account data, revoke device permissions and decline optional AI or location processing. Use Profile → Account, privacy and help to export or delete your account. See account deletion or contact support for access problems. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents by email.

This notice describes the current pilot. Store privacy declarations and final release review are still pending; no store approval is implied.
