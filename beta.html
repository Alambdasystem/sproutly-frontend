Your first growing story

  1. Explore the sample garden, or create your own account to save personal records.
  2. Add one real plant or seed project. Save a journal entry or complete a Plant Doctor conversation.
  3. Return the next day and a week later to record what changed.

Plant Doctor can work with photos, text and optional voice. AI guidance may be wrong; review it before acting. Call mode takes turns. Device microphone, speaker, permission, camera, keyboard and installation checks are still part of beta testing.

Volunteer for the beta

Email James to volunteer. Say whether you use Android, iPhone or web and where you found Sproutly. Participation is optional. A download or email alone is not counted as an installed user.

If you choose to help verify installation, share your app version, device/OS and whether you successfully opened the installed app. Screenshots are optional: hide names, emails and personal plant notes. Never send passwords or access tokens. We record reviewed beta evidence separately from store-reported installs; this page adds no analytics or advertising trackers.

Send useful feedback

Include the steps you took, what you expected and what happened. For Plant Doctor, check photo upload, camera cancellation or denial, keyboard open and closed, Continue to care guidance, live words in dictation, spoken replies and End. Report a failure rather than marking an untested step passed.
