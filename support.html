Using Plant Doctor voice

Open Plant Doctor → Talk to Plant Doctor, choose a plant and allow the requested speech and AI processing. Live dictation writes into the message field. Call mode takes turns: the microphone pauses while Doctor answers. End stops listening and playback; a question already accepted may still finish in saved history. Audio depends on device permissions, volume and speech-service availability.

Account controls

Open Profile → Account, privacy and help for export and deletion. Saving support notes inside the app stores a note in your account; it does not send an email. Use the email link for a response.

Sproutly is a plant-care pilot. AI suggestions can be incomplete or wrong. Inspect your plant and compare evidence before acting.
