Delete your Sproutly account

You can initiate deletion from the app or request it here without installing the mobile app.

Delete directly

  1. Open Sproutly and sign in.
  2. Choose Profile → Account, privacy and help.
  3. Export anything you want to keep.
  4. Open Delete this account, enter your current password, type DELETE, acknowledge the subscription notice and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email an account deletion request

Send the request to jameshill@alambda.com from your account email where possible. If you cannot access that address, explain the access problem. Ownership must be verified before deletion. Do not send your password. This link opens your email app; the request is sent only when you press Send there.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes live account records, photos, journal, seed projects, plans, conversations, saved support notes and sessions. Existing local backup snapshots expire through seven-snapshot rotation and are not instantly removed. Account deletion does not cancel any app-store subscription; store billing is managed separately. Paid pilot subscriptions are not currently enabled.
